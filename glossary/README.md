# Glossary

A–Z index of terms used across the repo. When you hit a word you don't recognize while reading a concept page or lab, this is faster than re-reading the whole page.

## What lives here

A single `terms.md` file with one short entry per term, organized A–Z. Each entry:

- Defines the term in one or two sentences.
- Links to the most relevant concept page for the full treatment.
- Notes synonyms or related terms (e.g., "*agent loop* — see also *ReAct loop*").

A glossary entry is **not** a substitute for a concept page. If a term needs more than two sentences, it gets its own concept page and the glossary entry links to it.

## Why a glossary

Agentic AI has accumulated jargon faster than convergent meanings. Terms like *agent*, *tool*, *context*, *retrieval*, *trace*, *evaluation* are used differently in different communities. The glossary picks **one** working definition for this repo and points to where each term is used.

## Convention

- **Bold** the term being defined.
- Use sentence case.
- Keep entries under 60 words.
- Always link to at least one concept or lab where the term appears in context.
- If a term has a precise mathematical meaning (e.g., *policy*, *belief state*), link to the corresponding `math-foundations/` page.

## Contributing

The glossary is one of the lowest-friction contributions:

- See a term in the repo with no glossary entry? Add one.
- See an entry that's vague or wrong? Improve it.
- See two terms used inconsistently across pages? Open an issue and we'll unify.

A glossary entry is usually a 5-minute PR.

> 🟢 The glossary is classified **stable**. Definitions change rarely; new entries land continuously.
