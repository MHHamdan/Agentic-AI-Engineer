# Labs and organizations

The Helix Lab is an academic research lab led by Dr. Aanya Rao. Its focus is
retrieval systems, with a recent emphasis on graph-based retrieval through the
Lattice Project. The Helix Lab collaborates closely with the Meridian Institute
and receives funding from the Beacon Foundation.

The Meridian Institute is a non-profit research organization. Dr. Tomas Vega is its
principal scientist, and Dr. Wei Chen is a research scientist there. Meridian runs
the Prism Project on multimodal retrieval and has a long-standing partnership with
the Helix Lab dating back to the original hybrid-search work by Vega and Rao.

Atlas Systems is an industry research lab. Dr. Priya Nair leads its Sentinel Project
on retrieval evaluation and safety. Atlas Systems funds Sentinel internally and
partners with the Cascade Consortium to publish shared benchmarks.

Northgate University is an academic institution. Prof. Samuel Okafor leads a group
there studying the theoretical foundations of ranking. Northgate has trained
several researchers now active across the field, including Lena Fischer and Wei
Chen, both of whom completed doctorates under Okafor.

The Cascade Consortium is a cross-organization body that standardizes retrieval
evaluation methodology. Its steering committee includes Samuel Okafor, and it is
advised by Aanya Rao and Tomas Vega. Members include the Helix Lab, the Meridian
Institute, and Atlas Systems. Cascade is funded by the Beacon Foundation.
