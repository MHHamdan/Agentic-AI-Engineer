# Publications

"Hybrid Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering" was authored by Tomas Vega and
Aanya Rao during their time together at the Meridian Institute. It introduced the
hybrid-search method that both the Lattice and Prism projects later built upon.

"Community Summarization for Global Retrieval" was authored by Lena Fischer and
Aanya Rao as part of the Lattice Project. It describes how graph communities are
detected and summarized to answer corpus-wide questions, and cites the earlier
hybrid-retrieval paper by Vega and Rao.

"Multimodal Dense Retrieval at Scale" was authored by Wei Chen and Tomas Vega for
the Prism Project. It extends dense retrieval to images and tables and builds on the
hybrid-retrieval foundation.

"Standardized Benchmarks for Retrieval Faithfulness" was authored by Priya Nair on
behalf of the Sentinel Project and the Cascade Consortium. It proposes the shared
evaluation metrics that the Lattice, Prism, and Sentinel projects report against.

"Theoretical Limits of Ranking" was authored by Samuel Okafor at Northgate
University. It is a foundational analysis cited by the Sentinel benchmark paper and
by the community-summarization work from the Lattice Project.
