# Collaborations

The longest-running collaboration in this ecosystem is between the Helix Lab and the
Meridian Institute. It began when Aanya Rao and Tomas Vega co-authored the original
hybrid-retrieval paper at Meridian, and it continues today: Rao now leads Helix while
Vega leads Prism at Meridian, and the two advise the Cascade Consortium together.

The Cascade Consortium is the main hub connecting the field. The Helix Lab, the
Meridian Institute, and Atlas Systems are all members. Cascade links the Lattice,
Prism, and Sentinel projects through a shared evaluation methodology, so an
improvement to the Sentinel benchmarks affects how all three projects report results.

Northgate University trains researchers who then spread across the other
organizations. Lena Fischer went from Okafor's group at Northgate to the Helix Lab,
and Wei Chen went from the same group to the Meridian Institute. This shared training
lineage is one reason the Helix and Meridian research styles remain compatible.

Atlas Systems plays the role of the industry partner. Priya Nair's path runs through
the ecosystem: she interned at Meridian under Vega before joining Atlas, and now her
Sentinel Project feeds evaluation methodology back into Cascade, closing the loop
between academic and industry work.
