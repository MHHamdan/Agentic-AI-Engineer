# Funding

The Beacon Foundation is the primary external funder in this ecosystem. It funds the
Lattice Project at the Helix Lab, the Cascade Consortium, and provides general
support to the Helix Lab itself. Beacon's funding priorities emphasize open
evaluation methodology and reproducible retrieval research.

The Meridian Institute funds the Prism Project from its own non-profit endowment
rather than through the Beacon Foundation, which keeps Prism's multimodal agenda
independent of Beacon's evaluation-first priorities.

Atlas Systems funds the Sentinel Project internally as an industry lab. Because
Sentinel contributes its benchmarks to the Beacon-funded Cascade Consortium, there is
an indirect link between Atlas's internal funding and Beacon's evaluation agenda.

A recurring theme across the ecosystem is that funding shapes collaboration: the
Beacon Foundation's support for both Lattice and Cascade is part of why the Helix Lab
sits at the center of the evaluation-methodology effort, while Meridian's independent
funding lets Prism pursue multimodal work that the others do not prioritize.
