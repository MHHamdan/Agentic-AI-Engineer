# Researchers

Dr. Aanya Rao leads the Helix Lab and is known for her work on retrieval systems.
She previously worked at the Meridian Institute, where she collaborated with
Dr. Tomas Vega on dense retrieval methods. Rao now sets the research direction for
the Lattice Project and co-advises the Cascade Consortium on retrieval quality.

Dr. Tomas Vega is a principal scientist at the Meridian Institute. He co-authored
the original work on hybrid search with Aanya Rao and now leads the Prism Project
on multimodal retrieval. Vega advises the Cascade Consortium on evaluation
methodology and mentors several doctoral students at Northgate University.

Dr. Lena Fischer joined the Helix Lab in 2024 after completing her doctorate at
Northgate University under Prof. Samuel Okafor. She works directly under Aanya Rao
on graph-based retrieval and is the lead engineer on the Lattice Project. Fischer's
research focuses on entity-relationship extraction and community detection.

Dr. Priya Nair is a staff researcher at Atlas Systems, an industry lab. She leads
the Sentinel Project on retrieval evaluation and safety, and partners with the
Cascade Consortium to standardize benchmarks. Nair previously interned at the
Meridian Institute under Tomas Vega.

Prof. Samuel Okafor is a professor at Northgate University. He advised both Lena
Fischer and Wei Chen during their doctorates. Okafor's group studies the
theoretical foundations of ranking, and he sits on the steering committee of the
Cascade Consortium.

Dr. Wei Chen is a research scientist at the Meridian Institute working on the Prism
Project alongside Tomas Vega. Chen completed a doctorate at Northgate University
under Samuel Okafor and specializes in multimodal embeddings.
