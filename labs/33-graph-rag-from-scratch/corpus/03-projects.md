# Projects

The Lattice Project is a graph-based retrieval effort housed at the Helix Lab.
Aanya Rao sets its direction and Lena Fischer is the lead engineer. Lattice builds
knowledge graphs from document corpora and answers global questions by summarizing
communities of related entities. It is funded by the Beacon Foundation.

The Prism Project is a multimodal retrieval effort at the Meridian Institute, led by
Tomas Vega with Wei Chen as a core contributor. Prism extends dense retrieval to
images and tables. It builds on the earlier hybrid-search work that Vega and Rao
published together.

The Sentinel Project is a retrieval evaluation and safety effort at Atlas Systems,
led by Priya Nair. Sentinel develops benchmarks for faithfulness and citation
correctness, and contributes its methodology to the Cascade Consortium. Sentinel is
funded internally by Atlas Systems.

The Cascade Consortium coordinates evaluation methodology across the Lattice, Prism,
and Sentinel projects. It does not build retrieval systems itself; instead it
defines the shared benchmarks and metrics the other projects report against. Cascade
is advised by Aanya Rao and Tomas Vega and funded by the Beacon Foundation.
